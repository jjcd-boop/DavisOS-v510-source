1. Ring-3 launch freeze — CORRECTED IN SOURCE
---------------------------------------------
Clicking FILES / WRITER / MATH LAB halted the machine (cli;hlt).
A. Syscalls/timer/exceptions ran on the process CR3; SYS_ALLOC walked unmapped pages.
   Files: Kernel/TrapExit.S, UserEntry.S, TimerEntry.S, Exceptions.S
   (DavisSwitchToKernelAddressSpace / DavisRestoreUserAddressSpaceIfCpl3)
B. Timer IRQ between mov cr3 and mov rsp in DavisResumePreparedUser. cli across the pair.
C. Handshake nested DesktopRuntime::PumpOnce. Now DesktopService::Tick + OwnerHasPresented.
   Files: Kernel/BootAppRunner.cpp, DxeServices.cpp, DxeServices.hpp

2. USB exports did not carry the repair — CORRECTED IN THIS EXPORT
------------------------------------------------------------------
v480 DAVISKRN.BIN (414,008 bytes) was flashed. A 385,392-byte blob was labelled
rebuilt but was not produced by clang here. v510 ships no flashable kernel; you relink.

3. Only three desktop icons — CORRECTED IN SOURCE

v510 Desktop.cpp draws a 2x8 grid of 16 apps including Pebble, Morrow, Forge.

4. Pebble / Morrow / Forge — CORRECTED IN SOURCE / APP IMAGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Morrow/Forge were valid DXE2 but only in the programs menu.
